# Cyber Trail: campaign chronicle (reconstruction)

**Read this first.** This is NOT the verbatim script. The original scene-by-scene narration was lost when this session's earlier context was compressed. This file is rebuilt from three things that survived: the GM notes written during play, the live save, and the list of the player's own messages in order. Everything marked **[confirmed]** comes from the notes or save. Everything marked **[player action]** is what Static did, taken from the player's messages. Narration details I no longer have are marked **[gap]**. Nothing here is invented to fill gaps.

The verbatim script still exists in the original chat on your screen. To keep it word for word, copy that chat out (select all and paste into a file), and use this file as the index to it.

## Premise and setup
- Player: **Static**, Netrunner. Claude is the GM and engine and played the rest of the crew and every NPC.
- Crew (picked by Claude, told through story, not listed to the player): **Odalys "Oda" Crane** (Solo), **Dust Reyes** (Nomad, drives), **Ines Okafor** (Medtech). Oda began as a Fixer and was refit to Solo because the party should not have a fixer. **Rail** is the NPC fixer. [confirmed]
- Hidden road patches (never named to the player): Red Sky, Bleed, Corps. [confirmed]
- Backstory: Static's sister **Wren** was lost in the Hollow Creek net-fire. A 3-beat pulse (long, short, short) in a packet is her signature. [confirmed]
- Route: The Badlands, then Dry Well Junction (km 330), Relay Ridge, Iron Pass, Salt Line, Outer Ring, Night City (km 1800).

11. **Achilles story.** Static told the "caveman story Achilles." [player action] [gap]
12. **Meeting Rail.** Static brought the crew in, shared a meal, and let Rail lead the formal meeting. [player action] Rail's offer: [confirmed] reach the loose AI fragment (a Bartmoss-style relic) before anyone else; **800 creds on arrival**; a chip with checkpoint codes; ping Rail from the second storm cell. 13. **Prep and sleep.** Static prepped his ICE and slept, looking back toward where home used to be. [player action] [gap]

15. **The jerky bag.** Static approached Rail one last time with a bag of synth jerky ("road snacks") and secretly hid a **control shard** inside it. Rail now holds the shard and link. [confirmed] The player noted the GM said too much in the first reply and wanted a short "what's this?" reply. [player action]
16. **Rolling out.** Static called "AutoGonks roll out!" and the crew drove on; he said, "They can scan all they want... We'll take it." (The "AutoGonks" line is the player's crew call.) [player action] [gap]
17. **The pulse.** Static reached into the net (a Perception roll) and answered the rhythm. **Critical success.** Static answered Wren's pulse and an AI replied: "YOU KEY LIKE HER / SHE IS NOT HERE. I AM LOOKING." Humanity fell from 50 to 47. [confirmed] A prep bonus of +2 was used.
18. **Gas station and mechanic Ferro.** The crew checked the gas station area, then visited the mechanic. Shielding was upgraded (Elec/Sec 20 vs 16), giving the crew +2 on the next EMP storm check. Cost 90 creds and 1 part. [confirmed] The player asked how much of the original Python was actually running underneath; the GM answered that the engine rolls, the GM narrates. The player later said they should have been rolling Perception regularly and that failures must have real consequences. [player action; also in memory]
19. **The mesh loom.** Static used a mesh loom action. [player action] [gap]
20. **Dry Well Junction diner.** The crew went in after Ines (she was taken). A corp van watched arrivals; a quiet man with an earpiece sat in the diner; things shifted while Static worked. [confirmed] Static placed a holo call, asked who the boss was, took scrub footage, passed the hat, de-escalated, then **jammed the van's control link (critical)**, diverting power and frying the diner's power; **Heat +2, creds -60 paid to Pilar**. The quiet man and the back-door man were dazed. **Ines was freed.** Static got +2 on the next Interface roll. Skipped IDs for time. Dust **crit-shot the gray van's tires**. Static hugged Ines. [confirmed and player actions]
21. **Breaching the van.** Static breached the van (**critical**) and fed it a **dummy network**: it thinks the truck went **east**. He learned its orders: RECOVER the sealed courier case (the party's package), same corp as the checkpoints. Humanity fell to 45 in the notes (46 in the save). The operator was locked out for about 20 minutes. [confirmed]
22. **The package.** Static asked how heavy it is and what it looks like on the outside, and asked whether an image could be generated. [player action] [gap on description] It was taken from a road fixer for a 100-cred advance, delivered to a dead-drop on the south side of Night City; it sits under the truck seat. Risk: second-checkpoint scanners. [confirmed]
